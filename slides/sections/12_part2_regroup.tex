\begin{frame}{Regroup 2: how good was the audit?}
  \vfill
  \begin{center}
    \slidepoint{How many problems did Claude \textbf{find}, and how many did \textbf{you verify}?}\\[1.1em]
    {\small What did it miss? Did it report anything that was not a problem?}\\[0.8em]
    {\small Which \textbf{model and setup} did you use? Did the choice change the audit?}\\[0.8em]
    {\small Which prompt or added context improved it? Were its citations accurate?}\\[1.1em]
    {\small Take one audit habit back to your own research.}
  \end{center}
  \vfill
  \ndlogonote{\scriptsize Regroup: 15 minutes; participants share first}
\end{frame}
\note{Participants speak first. Ask each person which model and setup they used and group the answers on the board (model, could it run code, could it open the references). Compare across the groups before revealing anything: who found more, and who checked more? Then use the private deck for the reveal and the comparison with a smaller model. Emphasize calibration: finding four and verifying three is a better result than accepting a long list of findings unread. Close on ask, inspect, verify, iterate.}
