\begin{frame}{Two things to check before you start}
  \vfill
  \begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{1.2em}
    \item \textbf{DoD / DoW funding?}\\
      Researchers with Department of Defense contracts are currently
      \textcolor{OkVermillion}{prohibited} from using Anthropic products ---
      Claude.ai, the API, Claude Code.\\[0.3em]
      {\small\color{Muted}Questions: \texttt{researchsecurity@nd.edu}}
    \item \textbf{Everyone else: ask your PI.}\\
      Export control, sponsor data terms, and IRB conditions differ
      \emph{per project}. What is fine on one is not automatically fine
      on another.
  \end{itemize}
  \vfill
  \takeaway{Thirty seconds of checking, once per project, before the tool touches anything that matters.}
\end{frame}
