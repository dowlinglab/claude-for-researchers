# Pacing notes

The two sessions each allow 35 minutes of presentation and 45 of hands-on work. Part 1 taught that generous work time, with Alex walking the room, is the strongest part of the design. These notes record where the lecture is long relative to that, and mark places to stop talking. Nothing here has been cut.
