\heading{Part 2: claims you can check}{Summary \hfill 12 October 2026}
\textbf{A plausible claim is a starting point.} Preserve its exact wording,
identify the evidence it needs, open that evidence, and only then choose a verdict.
Today's goal is three checked claims and one defensible revision.

\topic{A small audit you can reuse}
\textbf{Quote $\rightarrow$ locate $\rightarrow$ compare $\rightarrow$ judge $\rightarrow$ revise.}
For each claim, record: original quotation and section; evidence needed; evidence
actually opened and its location; comparison; verdict and reason; proposed or
applied revision. Checking three claims does not validate the whole report.

\textbf{Verdict} & \textbf{When to use it}\\
\midrule
Mismatch & Opened evidence contradicts the claim.\\
Cannot verify & Required evidence is missing or has not been opened.\\
Not comparable & Conditions differ, so a direct comparison is inappropriate.\\
Supported with limitations & Evidence supports narrower wording. State the limit.\\
Verified & All parts are directly supported by inspected evidence.\\
