\heading{Part 2 activity: check three claims}{45 minutes \hfill Follow these steps in order}
\textbf{Finish with:} three exact claims, evidence and verdicts, one revision,
and a saved handoff. No package extraction, extra papers, or PDF build required.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Open fresh evidence (0--5 min).} Read \code{docs/handoff.md}.
If a baseline exists, preserve any \code{results/recheck/} by renaming it to an
unused archive name, then run:\\
\code{python scripts/baseline.py compare}\par
Use \code{results/recheck/steady_states.csv}. If no baseline exists and the
starter is unchanged, run:\\
\code{python scripts/baseline.py capture}\\
Then use \code{results/steady_states.csv}. Record your evidence path. If the source was
changed before capture, record the limitation and ask for help; do not label new
results a pre-change baseline.

\item \textbf{Choose three claims (5--10 min).}
Open \code{report/audit_fallback.tex}. Paste one \textbf{numerical}, one
\textbf{citation-based}, and one \textbf{model/behavior} claim, with exact wording
and section names, into \code{docs/claim_evidence_audit.md}.
Ask the agent for candidates if needed, then select three before judging them.

\item \textbf{Check, then judge (10--30 min).} Give your agent:
\begin{quote}\small
Keep the three original quotations exact. State the evidence needed, open it,
and show its location. For every number, make a separate row: reported value,
artifact value, unit conversion, rounded comparison, result. For citations,
record the actual passage and what it supports, or ``notes only.'' Assign verdicts
only after comparing evidence. Keep revisions separate from quotations.
\end{quote}
Open the Woolf source using the directions on the front. If inaccessible, write
\textbf{notes only; primary-source check pending}. Do not verify from notes alone.
\textbf{You:} open at least one cited evidence location and check it yourself.
Record your confirmation separately. Use the verdict table on the front;
a sentence with several facts needs every part checked.

\item \textbf{Revise one claim (30--40 min).}
Apply one reviewed revision to the report, keeping the original quote in the
audit, or justify why all three can stay. Review \code{git diff}.
If human review is pending, record \textbf{applied draft; human review pending}
when the source changed, or \textbf{proposal only; not applied} otherwise.
Default today: \textbf{source-only; revised PDF not built}.

\item \textbf{Save and hand off (40--45 min).} Stage the audit and report:\\
\code{git add docs/claim_evidence_audit.md report/audit_fallback.tex}\par
Then use the save commands on the front for evidence and the five-item handoff.
Stage only existing files you used. Reconcile the audit and handoff with the
actual diff, build status, and commits. Save a \textbf{partial checkpoint} if
checks or human review remain pending.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Stop and check:}\par
\checkitem{Three quotes + verdicts} \checkitem{Evidence located}\par
\checkitem{Revision status accurate} \checkitem{Evidence + handoff committed}

\textbf{Stuck?} After five minutes, ask for help or pair up. If execution or source
access fails, record missing evidence honestly and continue the checks you can
do. At minute 40, save; do not start an extension.\par
\textbf{Regroup (15 min):} show one evidence location, the verdict it supports,
and your revision or unresolved question.\par

\code{python scripts/build_report.py --evidence results/recheck}
(use \code{results} for a first capture), then
\code{python scripts/build_report.py --check}. Save the figure and receipt;
update build status. Details: \code{activities/session_guide.md}.}
